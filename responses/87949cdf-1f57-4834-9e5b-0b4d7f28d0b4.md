Adding basic login protection (like a passcode or a simple 'Passcode-based session') is fairly straightforward with FastAPI. Full OAuth2 with external providers (like Google or GitHub) is significantly more complex because it requires registering an application on their developer portals.

If your goal is just to secure the LAN page so that only you can use it, I'd suggest starting with a simple 'Access Token' or a Basic Auth popup.
